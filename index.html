LOCAL TECH PATHWAY

Meet i.c.stars Kansas City

i.c.stars is an immersive technology workforce and leadership program that combines project-based learning, business and leadership skills, employer challenges, mentorship, and career support. It is especially relevant for motivated people interested in technology and problem-solving.

  • Official Kansas City location: 1111 Main St, Suite 450, Kansas City, MO 64105
  • Phone: 816-782-8397
  • Program details, eligibility, current cohort dates, and application requirements must be confirmed directly with i.c.stars.

Before you apply

Check fit, not just eligibility

Ask about weekly time commitment, paid internship terms, cohort start date, transportation, equipment, placement support, and what happens after graduation.

i.c.stars is one option—not the only route. If tech is not a fit, the pathways above include healthcare, trades, logistics, manufacturing, and office careers.

More local low-college training routes to compare

  • Great Jobs KC — eligible adults may access tuition-free career training; check current cohorts.
  • MCC SkillUP Missouri — ask about training support if you receive SNAP.
  • Workforce Partnership — Kansas-side career coaching and possible training funding.
  • Skill Up WYCO — ask about eligibility and approved training in Wyandotte County.
  • KCKCC Workforce Development — credit and non-credit certificates.
  • KansasWorks and Missouri ETPL — official training-provider directories.
  • Missouri Registered Apprenticeships — paid work, instruction, and industry credentials.
  • Accelerating Opportunity: Kansas — integrated adult education and career credentials with support services.

These links are starting points, not a claim that every program is accepting applications or free. Verify tuition, credential recognition, schedule, wages, and funding before enrolling.

Before you enroll, ask these five questions

  1. Is this exact certificate or course approved for workforce funding right now?
  2. Could I qualify for WIOA, SkillUP, Great Jobs KC, a scholarship, or employer-paid training?
  3. What is the full cost, including exam fees, tools, uniforms, books, transportation, and retakes?
  4. What jobs hire graduates, and what are realistic starting wages in Kansas City?
  5. Can I get help with childcare, transportation, equipment, internet, or lost work hours?

Important: Do not pay a private training provider before asking a workforce center whether the specific program can be funded. Eligibility and funding are never guaranteed by this directory.
