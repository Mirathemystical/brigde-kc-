# BRIDGE KC — Project Charter

## Mission
Help Kansas City residents find relevant career, training, and digital-skills resources and identify a realistic next step.

## Problem hypothesis
Resource information can be scattered across organizations and websites. People may need help understanding which option fits their goal and what to do first. Validate this hypothesis through interviews before expanding the product.

## Initial users
- Adults exploring a career change or first job
- People seeking digital-skills training
- Community navigators and workforce-support organizations

## Six-week outcome
A tested, accessible prototype; a small set of verified resources; documented user feedback; and a concise impact report.

## In scope
- Search and category filtering
- Goal-to-next-step guidance
- Resource details and contact links
- A lightweight progress checklist
- Feedback collection design and measurement plan

## Out of scope for the first iteration
- Eligibility decisions or guarantees
- Automated job placement
- Collection of sensitive personal data
- Unverified claims that a resource is free or available
- Production-grade AI recommendations

## Leadership commitments
1. Listen before building.
2. Share decisions and blockers openly.
3. Make work accessible and inclusive.
4. Verify information and label uncertainty.
5. Measure whether people reach useful next steps.

## Success indicators
- 5–8 discovery interviews completed
- At least 10 resource records reviewed, with source and verification date
- 5 user tests completed
- Users can identify a relevant resource and its next action
- Top usability issues prioritized and assigned

## Privacy and safety
Collect the minimum data necessary. Obtain consent for interviews and notes. Avoid storing sensitive details. Provide clear limits and a route to a human organization for complex questions.
