# BRIDGE KC — Discovery Interview Guide

## Opening
“I'm exploring how people find career, training, and digital-skills support. I'm testing a concept, not evaluating you. You can skip any question.”

Ask permission before taking notes or recording. Do not request immigration, medical, financial-account, or other sensitive details.

## Questions
1. Tell me about the last time you looked for a job, career training, or digital-skills help.
2. Where did you search first?
3. What was easiest about the process?
4. Where did you get stuck or lose confidence?
5. How did you decide whether a program was trustworthy and relevant?
6. What information did you need before contacting an organization?
7. What happened after you found a promising option?
8. What would make taking the next step easier?
9. What would make you reluctant to use a tool like BRIDGE KC?
10. If you could change one thing about the current process, what would it be?

## Follow-up prompts
- “Can you tell me more?”
- “What did you do next?”
- “How did that affect you?”
- “What would a good outcome have looked like?”

## After each interview
- Summarize the problem in the participant's own words.
- Separate observed facts from assumptions.
- Record barriers and workarounds without identifying details.
- Tag themes: discovery, trust, eligibility, cost, access, digital skills, follow-through.
- Do not treat one person's experience as representative of everyone.

## Consent reminder
Explain the project purpose, intended use of notes, who can access them, and how a participant can withdraw their input. Store notes securely and anonymize them.
