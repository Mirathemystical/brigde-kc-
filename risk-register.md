# BRIDGE KC — Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood / impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Outdated resource details | High / High | Verify with providers; display last-checked date; provide correction path |
| Users mistake prototype guidance for official advice | Medium / High | State limits clearly; link to providers; no eligibility guarantees |
| Sensitive personal data is collected | Medium / High | Minimize collection; no accounts in MVP; anonymize research notes |
| Biased or incomplete resource coverage | Medium / High | Consult diverse users and organizations; document gaps |
| Low user trust | Medium / High | Show source, date checked, cost and requirements where verified |
| Team scope grows too quickly | High / Medium | Maintain a must-have backlog and weekly definition of done |
| AI output is inaccurate | Medium / High | Keep recommendations rule-based initially; if AI is added, ground it in verified sources and provide human escalation |
| Accessibility barriers | Medium / High | Test keyboard navigation, contrast, mobile layout, and plain language |
| Metrics reward clicks rather than outcomes | Medium / Medium | Measure completed next steps and successful connections where consent allows |
