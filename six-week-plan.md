# BRIDGE KC — Six-Week Plan

## Week 1 — Listen and define
- Recruit 5–8 participants and 2–3 community organizations.
- Conduct discovery interviews.
- Define the highest-priority user problem and baseline measures.
**Deliverable:** Interview synthesis and refined problem statement.

## Week 2 — Verify and scope
- Identify 10–15 potentially useful resources.
- Verify provider, eligibility, cost, delivery mode, location, contact details, and date checked.
- Select the smallest useful user journey.
**Deliverable:** Resource inventory and prioritized backlog.

## Week 3 — Prototype
- Refine mobile and keyboard accessibility.
- Build the core search, category filter, and goal-to-next-step flow.
- Make demo data and limitations visible.
**Deliverable:** Clickable browser prototype.

## Week 4 — Test
- Run 5 moderated usability sessions.
- Ask participants to find a resource and explain their next step.
- Record task completion, confusion, and confidence.
**Deliverable:** Test notes and ranked usability issues.

## Week 5 — Improve and measure
- Fix the highest-impact issues.
- Re-verify resource listings.
- Review privacy, accessibility, and risk register.
- Compare outcomes against the initial success indicators.
**Deliverable:** Updated prototype and impact scorecard.

## Week 6 — Share and decide
- Prepare a short demo and lessons learned.
- Present evidence, limitations, and recommendations.
- Decide whether to continue, pivot, or stop.
**Deliverable:** Presentation and next-phase plan.

## Weekly team rhythm
- Monday: agree on one measurable outcome per person.
- Midweek: 15-minute blocker check.
- Friday: demo work, review evidence, and record decisions.

## Definition of done
A feature is done when it works on mobile and desktop, keyboard use is considered, content is understandable, assumptions are labeled, and a teammate has tested it.
